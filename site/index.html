Limits — read before quoting

  • "untracked" is CoinMarketCap's listing state, quoted from its map documentation: "registered cryptocurrency projects that are listed but do not yet meet methodology requirements to have tracked markets". It means listed, no CMC-tracked market. It does not mean never traded anywhere: Dinari dShares trade on Dinari's own venue, and some wrappers may sit in pools CMC does not index. The number is about CoinMarketCap's coverage of what CoinMarketCap calls tokenised — which is exactly why it can be re-derived.
  • 4 wrapper ids do not resolve on any public CMC surface (39318 SILVER, 39002 HOOD, 39839 QQQ, 42326 APLD). They are shown in their own bucket and excluded from every count.
  • The RWA endpoints need a key (keyless → 403 error 1005). The roster leg on this page is live through the site's proxy while its key has credits, otherwise the committed snapshot of 2026-09-18 22:16 UTC, labelled. The state leg is keyless and live for anyone.
  • The map's symbol filter rejects CMC's own dotted symbols (NVDA.D, AI.FRx — 38 wrappers). Those take their live state from /v2/cryptocurrency/info (active / inactive) and the map's finer word from the committed snapshot; each row says which.
  • The census is a daily series, not a history. Untracked map rows carry no dates; the series started 2026-09-18 and the delta panel shows what moved between the two latest days.
  • num_tokens is undocumented. It is shown as "declared by the registry" and never substituted for attached wrappers (Backed declares 1176, 772 are attached).
